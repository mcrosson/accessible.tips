## 'Her Doing' Ideas / Suggestions

### Attribution

Attribution goes to Her Doing. They sent a great breakdown on how they use icons and emoji to help organize notes, journal entries and more. This approach allows for easier searches and visualization of information.

The below is a lightly edited transcript of the information they provided and approved for publishing.

### General Icons

On icons, I have all the ones in my journal template (💛 for small good things, etc.), but I also have a list that can be scattered anywhere and I might want to find later, or I want to keep track of.

Such as
🌿 Dates [redacted] cut the lawn (so I know when, how often, and what the final total will be)
📙 Book recommendations
📚 Book finished. 😊
🍽️ Ate at least one good meal
👣 Walked for at least fifteen minutes
🏋️‍♀️ Exercised (other than walking) at least fifteen minutes
🤝 Reached out to at least one other person
📁 Do one thing your future self will thank you for
📖 Read for at least fifteen minutes
📻 Listened to a podcast
🤔 Studied at least fifteen minutes
📯 Played with a hobby for at least fifteen minutes
🛠️ Wrote for at least fifteen minutes
🪥 Basic personal hygiene
🧹 Basic house hygiene
🌜 Lights out by 10.00 pm 

(I have never accomplished all of these in one day. I'm not sure I have ever accomplished *half* in one day. Some I have *never* accomplished. But it is still useful to be aware of just how much I am or am not doing something. 😄)

### Journal Icons

My daily journal has sections:
💛 Small good things at the very top. 🥰
💭 Daily thoughts - whatever is going on / I want to write about. 
💡 Daily inspiration - quotes, ideas, book snippets - anything that made me stop and think, and especially that I will want to remember and/or build on
🌱 Story ideas - I write, so whenever I have an idea, I jot down here as quickly as I can, plus any details, backstory, possible directions it can take, whatever. It's nice for me to know when I had the idea and possibly what inspired it. This is only meant to be a paragraph or so. Sometimes I will write two or three pages - when that happens, I copy & paste it into a document, delete everything but the first paragraph (so I have a basic idea) and link to the full document. This is supposed to be my journal, so just the idea is enough - but I haven't lost anything.
🛐 I pray for people & this is where I record for whom and for what (and updates and answers)

### Additional Thoughts

I really wanted to make them easy to understand and remember - and some were tricky to develop - reading, book recommendations, and books finished, for example!

I already had the seedling for my story ideas and then months later the lawn-mowing thing came up & I had to keep track of that - and there is no lawn mower icon. 😄

Walking is what I am most likely to do - but I wanted another for anything else, whether inside (like yoga) or outside (swimming or going to a gym), so I wanted to acknowledge / keep track of both.

(I have never done yoga, nor am I a gym member - I am trying to think ahead to future needs & scenarios.)

Some of the others - reading, writing, playing with a hobby, listening to a podcast - these were all things that make up a good day for me. If I haven't done one or two, then hopefully it was because I had a good day filled with the others.

They are NOT a goal to reach - as I said, I have never done all of these in one day, and rarely even half. It's more of a record of the good ways I spend my days - kind of like your rose/thorn/bud idea.

And maybe it will change seasonally. Maybe in summer I will spend my days walking and in winter with yoga. Or I'll know if I spent a lot of time listening to podcasts, maybe it was a time I didn't have the spoons / strength to do anything else - and that's okay. I am still finding ways to be as active physically and mentally as I can be. 💪 🥰
